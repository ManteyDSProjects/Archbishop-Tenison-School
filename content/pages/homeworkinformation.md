Please find information regarding homework at Archbishop Tenison's in this booklet.  Here you will find timetables to support homework organisation as well as a guide to homework.

Please be advised that we are no longer using Satchel:One as our homework portal for Parents.  Homework will instead be listed as part of the facilities available in Bromcom.

Please ensure that you have logged into MCAS (My Child at School) on a desktop or via the App to set up your account and should you need support, please in the initial stages use this guide:  [https://www.archten.croydon.sch.uk/\_files/ugd/3094c8\_4c9a8c1649f241dbaa489699d41c179d.pdf](/documents/pages/homeworkinformation-4c9a8c.pdf)
