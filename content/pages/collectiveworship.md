All pupils and students at Archbishop Tenison’s take part in various acts of worship throughout the school week.

These take the form of:

- a weekly act of Collective Worship in the Hall

- a weekly act of Collective Worship for each year group at St Matthew’s Church (or a Sixth Form act of Collective Worship in the Hall)

- a daily act of Morning Prayer

- prayer at the start of each lesson, which changes each half term.

- an Follow Up Task to the Collective Worship act in morning Tutor Time once a week.

I[n our acts of Collective Worship in the hall we are currently looking at the life, miracles and teaching of Jesus as told in Luke’s gospel.](/documents/pages/collectiveworship-n-our-acts-of-collective-worship-in-the-ha.pdf)

As part of Collective Worship in the hall,  with years 7-10 there is also a time of sung worship, which you can experience in the video below.

As a Church of England school we undertake to give all pupils and students at Archbishop Tenison’s the opportunity to take part in an Anglican church service on each of the following occasions:

- at the start of the school year in September

- at Christmas (at the end of the Autumn Term)

- at Easter (at the end of the Spring Term)

- at the end of the school year in July

We are also committed to holding a Eucharist for each year group once in each academic year. T[hese Eucharists follow the following pattern:](/documents/pages/collectiveworship-hese-eucharists-follow-the-following-patte.pdf)

On Founder’s Day (usually the last Friday before the first May Bank Holiday) the whole school attends a special service at Croydon Minster to mark the anniversary of the foundation of the school by Archbishop Thomas Tenison and to look forward together to the next year in the life of the school.
