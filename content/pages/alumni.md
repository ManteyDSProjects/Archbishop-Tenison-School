**Croydon Tenisonians - Archbishop Tenison’s School Alumni**

At Archbishop Tenison’s School, we value our connection with our former students and staff members.

We are interested to see how our student leavers have developed as individuals and what they went on to do and contribute to society beyond their school years.

Registering as a Croydon Tenisonian will enable us to reconnect with former students and staff members at alumni reunions, other school events and celebrations. It will also help us to offer our current students invaluable insight, encouragement and perspective on life beyond Tenison’s.

Sharing experiences of university life, gap years, apprenticeships and working life with our current students provides an invaluable source of inspiration and builds on understanding and encourages interest.

We would like to celebrate the achievements and experiences of our alumni with students, staff, and the whole school community – both past and present and would encourage you to register.

Data collected will be stored and used in accordance with the School’s Privacy Policy.
