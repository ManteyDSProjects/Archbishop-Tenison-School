In his grace, God has given us different gifts for doing certain things well (Romans 12:6)

At Archbishop Tenison's we define a child as gifted if they are achieving highly across their subjects, whereas, a child could be talented in a particular subject. We believe that all children should have the opportunity to nurture these gifts and talents.
