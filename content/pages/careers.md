**What is the right Career for me?**

If you are unsure about your future, start your search through a quiz to help you think about your options-

Take the ‘[Buzz Quiz](https://www.ucas.com/careers/buzz-quiz)’ from UCAS.

Or this [skills assessment](https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/skills-assessment) from the National Careers Service.

Interested in the Healthcare services? Maybe a career in the [NHS](https://www.healthcareers.nhs.uk/findyourcareer) is right for you.

[BBC Bitesize Careers](https://www.bbc.co.uk/bitesize/careers) offers advice and information about careers in an easy-to digest format.

[ICould](https://icould.com/) offers real-life stories to inspire your future career, as well as a careers quiz and advice about that do next.

[Learn Live](https://learnliveuk.com/my-week-of-work/) offers live webinars with careers and the world of work, which you can access from the comfort of your laptop.

Interested in the labour market? Click this link to [Labour Market Information For All](https://www.lmiforall.org.uk/) to search potential career avenues.

**Skills**

There’s been a pandemic and you haven’t managed to find an internship? Why not try a [virtual internship](https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/careers-advice/how-to-find-a-virtual-internships/)- this government website can help you find one.

Have no idea how to start writing a CV? Check out this [wikihow](https://www.wikijob.co.uk/content/application-advice/cvs/how-write-cv) on how to do that.

Shakey on you Employability skills? Check out [Youth Employment](https://www.youthemployment.org.uk/) for some options on free training and advice.

Interested in developing skills for employment or education? Try [Barclays Life Skills](https://barclayslifeskills.com/), which gives millions of people the skills, knowledge and confidence they need for work with tools, tips and learning resources.

**Apprenticeships, University and post-18 careers**

Is an apprenticeship right for you? Search this [government database](https://www.gov.uk/apply-apprenticeship) for apprenticeships that might suit your skills

Thinking about applying to University? Try this website that helps you find [University taster days.](https://www.unitasterdays.com/)

Compare different university courses with  this handy government tool- [Discover Uni](https://discoveruni.gov.uk/).

For information about apprenticeships and university, try the [UCAS website](https://www.ucas.com/) itself

Looking for apprenticeships, graduate schemes and jobs? Try [Career Map](https://careermap.co.uk/), which consolidates all this opportunities in to one easy-to-use website

Maybe you want to continue learning, but don’t want to go to University? Why not do a [T Level](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/introduction-of-t-levels/introduction-of-t-levels)? These offer on-the-job training in practical subjects and real world careers.

If you are unsure whether university is right for you, [FutureLearn](https://www.futurelearn.com/courses/career-planning-and-your-degree) will help you to identify how to make the most of a degree to better your employability after graduation.

If you'd like to find apprenticeships you can try either the [government website](https://www.gov.uk/apply-apprenticeship) or [Uptree](https://uptree.co/)- subscribe to their newsletter for up-to-date information.

**Extra-Curricular Activities**

Apply for a place on a [Sutton trust programme](https://www.suttontrust.com/our-programmes)- the Sutton Trust supports young people from less advantaged backgrounds to access leading universities and careers. In partnership with universities and employers, they run engaging programmes that give students practical advice and leave them feeling inspired and more confident about their future.

Their programmes are completely free to attend. All costs - including travel, accommodation, food and activities – are paid for by the Trust and their partners.
