**Tenacious Together -  the Film**

Tenacious Together is an initiative which makes the connection between learning and life; between school and society.  Over a 7-week period, pupils and students worked with experts in their field to learn, explore and develop a range of thought-provoking projects.

This unique collaborative learning experience challenged and stretched pupils to learn, work as a team and engaged them in ways in which day-to-day school cannot.  Exploring a variety of different mediums including music, drama, spoken word, interviews, science, photography and art, pupils expressed what tenacity means to them.

The Tenacious Together Evening Event showcased the dedication of the Tenacious Together team.

The Tenacious Together Film documents pupils’ and students’ behind the scenes journeys throughout this project and celebrates the outcomes.  We now invite you to experience what our pupils have discovered through this project.
