Welcome back to a new academic year at Archbishop Tenison's. It has been wonderful to see our corridors and classrooms full of life again, and I want to thank our whole community, students, staff, and families, for the warm start to the term.

This year brings some exciting developments, including continued progress on our Vision 312 campaign and a renewed focus on academic excellence across every year group.

As always, our doors are open to any parent or carer who wishes to discuss their child's progress or raise a question about school life.
