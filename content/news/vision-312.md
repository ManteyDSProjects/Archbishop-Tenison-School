---
title: "Vision 312: Our £1 Million Campaign to Transform Our Site"
date: "2026-06-15"
excerpt: "We have launched Vision 312, an ambitious fundraising campaign to modernise our facilities for the next generation of students."
---

We are proud to launch Vision 312, an ambitious campaign to raise £1 million in donations over the next year to modernise our school facilities.

The campaign will fund science lab upgrades, a new independent learning centre with library and computer facilities, enhanced arts spaces, a respite support centre, and improved Sixth Form classrooms.

This bold project will give our students contemporary resources and environments that support academic achievement and personal development across all areas of the curriculum.

We will share updates on our progress throughout the year. Thank you to everyone in our community who has already pledged their support.
